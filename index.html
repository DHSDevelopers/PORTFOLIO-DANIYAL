01 — About & Vision

Lead Developer
& Web Architect

I'm Daniyal Hasan — a 15-year-old founder and lead developer from Maharashtra, India. I run DHS Developers, where I design and build high-performance web applications, single-page tools and AI-driven interfaces with a heavy focus on aesthetic precision.

Most of what I make starts as a blank file and ends as something people can open, use and feel. I care about the milliseconds — how quickly a page becomes useful, how a button answers your finger, how a layout holds together at 360 px and on a 4K display. My work sits where engineering meets art direction: clean code underneath, atmosphere on top.

Outside the editor you'll find me on a pull-up bar, halfway through an absurdly large cube, or pausing a football match to watch the off-ball run nobody else noticed. Same patience, different arena.

“I build interfaces the way I train — slow reps, clean form, no shortcuts.”
